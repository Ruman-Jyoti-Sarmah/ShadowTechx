Start with the task

Define what should improve—and what must stay controlled.

Before selecting a model or building an assistant, identify the user, source material, expected result, failure modes, and the point where a person needs to review or decide.

AI systems can be uncertain and depend on their providers, prompts, and data. A responsible project should evaluate accuracy, access controls, privacy implications, cost, and fallback behavior against its actual use case.

Potential use cases

  • Search and question-answering over approved documents.
  • Drafting or classifying routine information for review.
  • Guided assistants for a narrowly defined workflow.
  • Connecting AI features to existing tools with explicit permissions.
