Privacy notice

What happens to the information you send us, in plain English.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This is a plain summary of what this website does with your information. It isn't legal advice, and if we ever sign a project agreement with you, that document governs the work itself.

What the contact form collects

Your name, email address, what you're building, your project details, and a phone number if you choose to add one. A timestamp gets attached automatically so we know when you sent it.

Where it goes

Straight into our email inbox through EmailJS, a form service that runs in your browser. Nothing is stored in a database on this site, because this site doesn't have one.

What we do with it

We read it, reply to it, and if it turns into a project, we keep it for the project record. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or add you to a mailing list you didn't ask for.

Other things the site loads

Fonts from Google Fonts, icons from Font Awesome, and images from Unsplash. Those services see your IP address and browser details when they load. Their own privacy policies apply to that, not ours.

Asking us to delete something

Message us from the contact page with the email address you used and say you want your enquiry removed. We'll delete it from our inbox and confirm when it's done.

Changes to this page

If the way the site handles information changes, we update this page and the date above. We don't send notifications about it.

We can't control or promise anything about how EmailJS, Google or Font Awesome handle data on their own systems. If that matters for your situation, read their policies before sending us anything.
