Privacy notice

How information submitted through the Shadow Techx project inquiry form is handled.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This notice describes the information involved when you use this website. It is a plain-language summary, not legal advice. Project-specific arrangements may be described separately in a written agreement.

Information you submit

If you use the project inquiry form, it asks for your name, email address, project or product name, project details, and optionally your phone number. The form adds a submission time and sends the information through the website's configured EmailJS service so Shadow Techx can respond.

How inquiry information is used

Information submitted through the form is used to review and respond to your inquiry and, where relevant, discuss a possible project. Do not submit passwords, payment card information, or other sensitive credentials through the form.

Service providers and website resources

The form depends on EmailJS. The website also loads resources such as web fonts and icon libraries from external providers. Those providers may receive technical request information when your browser connects to their services; their own privacy notices govern those services.

Retention and requests

Retention and handling may depend on the systems used to receive and respond to an inquiry. To ask about a particular submission or request its deletion, contact Shadow Techx through the contact page and identify the email address used. We will review the request in light of applicable obligations and provider capabilities.

Changes to this notice

This page may be updated when the website or inquiry workflow changes. The date above indicates the latest stated revision.

This website does not make a promise about third-party provider retention or security practices. Review the relevant provider policies before submitting information.
