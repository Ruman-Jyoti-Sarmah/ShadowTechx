GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

What can Shadow Techx build?
Our service areas include websites and web apps, Android and iOS apps, custom business software, desktop applications, e-commerce platforms, and AI-enabled workflows. We’ll confirm the best fit after understanding your requirements.
Which technologies do you use?
The stack depends on the product. Options may include React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Flutter, Swift, Kotlin, Electron, and relevant cloud or payment services.
How are timeline and project scope decided?
We review your goals, features, integrations, and constraints first. The proposed scope, milestones, schedule, and pricing are then agreed before implementation begins.
Who owns the source code, and what happens after launch?
Source-code ownership, repository access, handover, and any maintenance or warranty support are set out in the project agreement so expectations are clear before work starts.
Can you connect a new product to our existing tools?
Where suitable APIs or integration options are available, we can assess connecting your product to existing systems and third-party services as part of the project scope.
