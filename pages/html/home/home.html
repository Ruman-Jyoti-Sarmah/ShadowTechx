HOW A PROJECT RUNS

What working with us actually looks like

No account managers, no weekly status decks. Four steps, and you know where things stand at each one.

Start with a message
  1. 01 Step 1:

    You tell us the problem

    A call or a long message is fine. What are you trying to do, who is it for, and what's getting in the way today? We ask questions before we quote anything.

    Usually 1–2 days

  2. 02 Step 2:

    We write the scope down

    A short document: what's included, what isn't, what it costs and when it lands. You read it, we change what's wrong, then we start.

    Fixed price, agreed up front

  3. 03 Step 3:

    You see it while it's being built

    A staging link from week one. You can click through it any time and tell us what's off — much cheaper than finding out at the end.

    Updates every few days

  4. 04 Step 4:

    We hand it over properly

    The repo, the hosting, the domain, the admin logins — all yours. Plus 30 days of free bug fixes after launch, because something always turns up.

    Everything in your name

We don't publish client quotes until the client has approved them. If you want to hear from someone we've worked with, ask us and we'll connect you.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions people actually ask

What can you actually build?
Websites, web apps, Android and iOS apps, internal tools and dashboards, online stores, desktop software, and small AI features like document search. If it's outside that, we'll tell you instead of pretending.
What do you build with?
Mostly React, Next.js, TypeScript and Node.js, with PostgreSQL or MongoDB behind them. Flutter or React Native for mobile, Electron for desktop. We pick what fits the project, not what's trending.
How long does a project take?
A 3–6 page website is usually 2–3 weeks. A web app or mobile app is 6–12 weeks depending on scope. We give you a date in writing before we start, and we tell you early if it's going to slip.
Who owns the code after launch?
You do. The GitHub repo, the hosting account, the domain — all transferred to you at handover. You can keep working with us or take it to any other developer. No lock-in.
Can you connect to the tools we already use?
Usually yes — if the tool has an API or a webhook, we can connect to it. That covers things like payment gateways, CRMs, Google Sheets, WhatsApp Business and most accounting software.
