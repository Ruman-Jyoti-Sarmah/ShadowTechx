How projects run

What happens after you message us

Same process every time, whether it's a three-page site or a six-month app.

01 / Talk

We ask questions first

Who uses it, what they do today, what's broken. We don't quote until we understand the job — a wrong quote wastes everyone's time.

02 / Agree

You get it in writing

Scope, price, and a delivery date in one short document. You read it, we fix what's wrong, then work starts.

03 / Build

You watch it happen

A staging link from week one. Click through it whenever you like and tell us what's off while it's still cheap to change.
