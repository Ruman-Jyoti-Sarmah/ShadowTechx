About Shadow Techx

Small team. Thoughtful digital work.

We help turn practical ideas into useful websites, software, and digital experiences—through clear collaboration and careful engineering.

Meet us about your project

Our approach

Build the right thing, then build it well.

Shadow Techx was started by Ruman Jyoti Sarmah and Amlan Phukan to make digital product work more direct and understandable for the people commissioning it.

We work closely with clients to understand what they need, what they already have, and what a useful first release should do. That context guides the design, technology choices, and delivery plan.

Our goal is straightforward: dependable work, transparent decisions, and a handover that leaves clients clear about what has been built.

A direct partnership

Work with the people building your product.

Project conversations, scope, and delivery plans are handled directly with the Shadow Techx team.

We agree responsibilities, timelines, and next steps together before work begins.
