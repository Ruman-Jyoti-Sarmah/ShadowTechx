About Shadow Techx

Technology & digital solutions. Built as one team.

Shadow Techx is a digital solutions company based in Assam, India. Engineers, designers, video and 3D artists, AI specialists and business people — small enough to stay direct, broad enough to take on the whole job.

Tell us about your project

Why we started

We got tired of good ideas getting overpriced.

Shadow Techx started in Assam after watching small businesses pay agency money for work that was built by someone they never spoke to, then handed a login and left alone. The scoping, the design and the code were spread across three companies, and nobody who sent the quote ever wrote a line of it.

We built the opposite. One company, every skill in-house — engineering, design, motion and 3D, AI, and the business side that keeps a project moving. And a founder who stays on your project from the first message to the handover.

Broad enough to cover the whole job, still small enough that you know who's building your thing.

What you get

Straight answers, and code you own.

We'll tell you if your idea is too big for your budget, if a cheaper option would do the job, or if we're not the right fit. Better to say it on day one than in month three.

At handover, the repo, hosting and domain are all in your name. No lock-in, no hostage situation.

How we behave

Three promises we actually keep

Not values on a wall. Things that show up in every project.

You get a real answer

Messages get replied to within a working day. If we're stuck on something, you hear that too — not silence until the deadline.

The price doesn't move

We quote after we understand the work, then stick to it. If you add features mid-project, we tell you what it costs before we build it.

You can leave any time

Everything lives in your accounts. If you want to move to another developer after launch, we'll help with the transfer instead of making it hard.
